AGGS Tennis

How to play

Tennis is a fun, fast-paced sport played with a ball and rackets
over a net. It can be played as singles (1 vs 1) or doubles (2 vs 2). The goal is to hit the ball over the net and land it inside the
opponent’s court to score points.

Rules

To ensure fair play and smooth matches, players must follow key rules such as serving diagonally into the correct service box, allowing the ball to bounce no more than once before returning it, keeping the ball within the court boundaries, and avoiding contact with the net during play.

  • Serve diagonally into the opponent’s service box.
  • The ball can bounce once before being returned.
  • The ball must land inside the court boundaries.
  • Do not touch the net during play with your body or the racket.

How to play

In tennis, players score points by winning rallies; games are scored love, 15, 30, 40, with a player needing to win by two points if the score reaches deuce. Players serve from the right side when their score is even and from the left when it’s odd. A set is usually won by the first player to reach six games with a two-game advantage.

  • Points are scored love, 15, 30, 40.
  • Win by 2 points after reaching deuce.
  • A point is won on every rally.
  • Serve from the right on even scores, left on odd scores.
